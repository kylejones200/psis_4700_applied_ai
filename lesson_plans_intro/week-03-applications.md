## Gradable Activities

### Discussion: AI Applications in Your Domain (25 points)
- **Initial Post** (200 words minimum): Name a task in your field that is repetitive, data-rich, and high-impact. Describe how AI might be applied to this task. Identify the top feasibility risk (data, technical, organizational, or ethical) and explain why it's the primary concern.
- **Reply** (75 words minimum): Respond to one peer's post, either suggesting a mitigation strategy for their identified risk, or sharing a similar example from your experience.
- **Due Dates**:
  - Initial post: End of Week 3 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
  - Reply to peer: 48 hours after initial post deadline
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Clear task description and AI application idea (30%)
  - Identification and explanation of top feasibility risk (40%)
  - Thoughtful engagement with peers in reply (30%)

### Journal: Domain Proposal Analysis (75 points)
- **Task**: Draft a 2-3 page domain proposal analyzing an AI application in your field
- **Required Sections**:
  1. **Problem Statement** (1 paragraph): What problem would AI solve? Who are the stakeholders?
  2. **Proposed Solution** (1 paragraph): How would AI be applied? What type of AI approach?
  3. **Feasibility Analysis** (1-2 paragraphs): 
     - Data readiness: What data is needed? Is it available?
     - Technical feasibility: What are the technical requirements?
     - Value/cost: What's the ROI or value proposition?
  4. **Risks and Mitigation** (1 paragraph): Top 2-3 risks and how to address them
  5. **Success Metrics** (2-3 bullet points): How would you measure success? What KPIs?
- **Format**: PDF or Word document, 2-3 pages
- **Due**: End of Week 3 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Clear problem statement and proposed solution (25%)
  - Thorough feasibility analysis (35%)
  - Thoughtful risk identification and mitigation (25%)
  - Appropriate success metrics (15%)

## Instructor Notes
- **Module 3.1**: Keep focus on feasibility and ethics; avoid tech rabbit holes. Provide domain-specific exemplars for varied majors.
- **Module 3.2**: Encourage students to think critically about both opportunities and challenges. Emphasize that not every problem needs AI.
- **Differentiation**: Provide domain-specific exemplars for varied majors. Offer optional deeper dives for technical learners (integration diagrams).
- **Asynchronous Considerations**: Provide clear case study templates. Create discussion forums organized by domain interest. Offer curated reading lists by major/field.
