# Weekly Reading Assignments - Introduction to Applied AI

This document provides recommended readings for each week of the 8-week course. Readings are categorized as **Required** (all students must complete) or **Optional** (for deeper exploration).

---

## Week 1: What Is AI?

### Required Readings
1. **Course Reference**: `course_materials/reference/what_is_ai.md` (or PDF)
   - Core definitions and concepts
   - Estimated reading time: 15 minutes

2. **Course Reference**: `course_materials/reference/ai_terms_plain_english.md` (or PDF)
   - Glossary of key terms (review relevant sections)
   - Estimated reading time: 20 minutes

3. **Course Slides**: `webslides/week01_what-is-ai/01-main-lesson.md`
   - Complete slide deck for the week
   - Estimated reading time: 45-60 minutes

### Optional Readings
- **External**: "A Brief History of AI" - Any accessible overview article (e.g., from MIT Technology Review, Harvard Business Review, or similar)
- **External**: Recent news article about AI applications in your field of interest

### Interactive Activities (Required)
- Explore a chatbot tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or similar) - hands-on practice
- Explore a text-to-image tool (DALL-E, Midjourney, or similar) - hands-on practice

## Reading Strategy Notes

### For Students
- **Required readings** should be completed before attempting graded activities
- **Estimated times** are approximate - adjust based on your reading pace
- Use **optional readings** to deepen understanding in areas of interest
- **Interactive activities** are essential - don't skip hands-on practice
- Reference the **AI Terms Glossary** whenever you encounter unfamiliar terms

### For Instructors
- Consider providing direct links to external readings in your LMS
- Create discussion prompts that connect readings to activities
- Encourage students to share interesting optional readings they discover
- Provide alternative formats (audio, video summaries) for accessibility
- Consider creating reading guides or study questions for complex topics

---

## Accessibility Notes
- All course reference materials are available in both markdown (.md) and PDF formats
- PDF versions are located in `course_materials/reference/pdfs/`
- External readings should be checked for accessibility (screen reader compatibility, alt text, etc.)
- Consider providing transcripts or summaries for video content
