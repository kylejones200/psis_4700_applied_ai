## Gradable Activities

### Discussion: Ethics and Stakeholders (25 points)
- **Initial Post** (200 words minimum): Who is impacted by your chosen use-case (from Week 3) beyond the direct user? Identify at least 3 stakeholder groups. For each, explain one potential risk they might face. Then, name one concrete safeguard you would implement and explain why it addresses a key risk.
- **Reply** (75 words minimum): Respond to one peer's post, either suggesting an additional safeguard, identifying a stakeholder they might have missed, or discussing a tradeoff in their proposed safeguard.
- **Due Dates**:
  - Initial post: End of Week 4 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
  - Reply to peer: 48 hours after initial post deadline
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Clear stakeholder identification and risk analysis (40%)
  - Concrete safeguard proposal with rationale (40%)
  - Thoughtful engagement with peers in reply (20%)

### Activity: Risk Assessment and Safeguard Plan (75 points)
- **Task**: Complete a 1-2 page risk assessment and safeguard plan for your domain proposal (from Week 3)
- **Required Sections**:
  1. **Stakeholder Map** (1 paragraph or visual): Who is impacted? Include direct users, indirect users, and affected communities.
  2. **Risk Assessment** (1-2 paragraphs): Identify top 3 risks using likelihood × impact framework. For each risk, explain:
     - What is the risk?
     - Who is affected?
     - What is the likelihood and impact?
  3. **Safeguard Plan** (1-2 paragraphs): For each of the 3 risks, propose a specific safeguard. For each safeguard, explain:
     - What is the safeguard?
     - How does it address the risk?
     - Who is responsible for implementing it?
  4. **Reflection** (2-3 sentences): What tradeoffs exist in your safeguard choices? What remaining risks should be monitored?
- **Format**: PDF or Word document, 1-2 pages
- **Due**: End of Week 4 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Complete stakeholder mapping (20%)
  - Thorough risk assessment with likelihood/impact (30%)
  - Specific, actionable safeguard plan (35%)
  - Thoughtful reflection on tradeoffs and remaining risks (15%)

## Instructor Notes
- **Module 4.1**: Keep examples grounded; avoid abstract ethics without application. Provide non-technical and technical examples of bias.
- **Module 4.2**: Encourage specific, testable safeguards with clear owners. Help students understand that perfect solutions don't exist—there are always tradeoffs.
- **Differentiation**: Provide non-technical and technical examples of safeguards. Offer optional deep-dive on measurement of fairness metrics.
- **Asynchronous Considerations**: Provide clear templates and frameworks. Create discussion forums that encourage peer learning. Offer office hours or Q&A forum for complex ethical questions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Provide readings with accessible formatting
- Ensure slide contrast and readability
- Provide alternative formats for all activities
- Ensure videos have captions/transcripts
- Consider diverse perspectives in examples and case studies
