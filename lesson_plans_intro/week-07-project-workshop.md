## Gradable Activities

### Discussion: Project Progress and Risks (25 points)
- **Initial Post** (200 words minimum): What is the one result that will make your demo credible? Describe your top 3 project risks. For each risk, explain one experiment or test you could run this week to reduce that risk. Share one risk you've already retired and how you did it.
- **Reply** (75 words minimum): Respond to one peer's post, either suggesting an additional experiment, sharing a similar risk, or offering encouragement.
- **Due Dates**:
  - Initial post: End of Week 7 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
  - Reply to peer: 48 hours after initial post deadline
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Clear identification of credible result (25%)
  - Thoughtful risk analysis with experiments (50%)
  - Thoughtful engagement with peers in reply (25%)

### Activity: Updated Project Brief and Demo Plan (75 points)
- **Task**: Submit an updated project brief and demo preparation plan
- **Required Components**:
  1. **Updated Project Brief** (2-3 pages): Update your project from earlier weeks with:
     - Refined problem statement (feasible, ethical, valuable)
     - Acceptance criteria: What must your project do to be successful?
     - Evaluation plan: How will you measure success?
     - Top 3 risks and how you're addressing them
     - Experiment results: What did you test? What did you learn?
  2. **Demo Plan** (1-2 pages): Prepare for your final presentation with:
     - Demo narrative/story: What's the flow? What's the key message?
     - Key results to highlight: What makes your demo credible?
     - Contingency plan: What if something doesn't work during demo?
     - Timeline: How will you structure your 5-minute presentation?
- **Format**: PDF or Word document, 3-5 pages total
- **Due**: End of Week 7 (Sunday 11:59 PM)
- **Evaluation Criteria**:
  - Complete updated project brief with all sections (40%)
  - Clear acceptance criteria and evaluation plan (25%)
  - Thoughtful demo plan with narrative (25%)
  - Appropriate contingency planning (10%)

## Instructor Notes
- **Module 7.1**: Keep teams focused on outcomes, not tooling. Help students identify realistic risks and design quick, actionable experiments.
- **Module 7.2**: Emphasize that a good demo tells a story. Help students focus on what makes their project credible and valuable.
- **Differentiation**: Provide technical/non-technical experiment examples. Offer optional 1:1 instructor consults (via office hours or Q&A forum).
- **Asynchronous Considerations**: Provide clear templates and checklists. Create discussion forums for peer feedback. Offer video submission option for demo rehearsal (optional). Set up Q&A forum for project questions. Provide examples of good project briefs and demos.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Allow alternative demo formats (written, video, interactive, etc.)
- Ensure captioning resources are available
- Provide clear submission guidelines
- Offer flexible submission formats
- Ensure all templates and materials are accessible
