## What Bias Looks Like

- **Sampling bias:** the data excludes key groups
- **Label bias:** human labels reflect assumptions
- **Algorithmic bias:** model design favors certain outcomes

## Ethics Checklist

- **Representation:** Does data reflect every population served?
- **Transparency:** Can you explain the decision process clearly?
- **Ownership:** Does a third party train on sensitive data?

> Following systematic processes reduces mistakes and improves outcomes. Established workflows capture lessons learned.

## Practical Mitigation Steps

- Audit datasets for imbalance
- Test for demographic differences in output
- Monitor drift over time
- Document model purpose and limits
