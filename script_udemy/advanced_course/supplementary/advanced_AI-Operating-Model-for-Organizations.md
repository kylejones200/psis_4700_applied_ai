Projects end, but capabilities persist. This shift defines maturity. An operating model connects people, data, and process—it defines how AI is built and scaled. Strategy defines direction, data provides the foundation, people execute, and process connects everything. All must align.

Organizations move from experiment to adoption to scale to optimization. Each stage builds on the previous. Clear ownership prevents uncontrolled systems, and tracking ensures accountability. A well-defined utility establishes roles and responsibilities so that everyone knows who owns what and how work flows through the organization. When these elements come together, AI becomes a sustainable capability rather than a series of isolated projects.
