# Module 1 Content Map
## Applied Artificial Intelligence for the Workplace

## Section 1: Module Overview

This module introduces applied artificial intelligence as it appears in real organizations. Students learn how applied AI differs from research, hype, and experimentation. The module establishes shared language and mental models used throughout the course. Students practice framing a real workplace problem as a candidate for applied AI.
