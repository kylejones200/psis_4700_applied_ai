## Content Organization Rules

1. All assets must be mapped to a module and lesson objective.
2. Assets without clear mapping become reference or enrichment material.
3. Primary lesson content lives in the LMS, not in external files.
4. Instructional support assets are labeled Required or Optional.
5. Reference assets can appear across multiple modules.

## Update Process

1. Update the module content map document first.
2. Then update the LMS placement.
3. Treat the content map as the single source of truth.
