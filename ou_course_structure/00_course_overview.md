# Applied Artificial Intelligence for the Workplace

## Course Title
Applied Artificial Intelligence for the Workplace

## Course Format
Eight week asynchronous online course designed for adult learners.
All content stands alone and requires no live instruction.

## Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students can:

1. Frame a workplace problem as an applied AI use case with clear constraints and success criteria.
2. Evaluate AI techniques and tools based on data availability, cost, risk, and operational fit.
3. Assemble simple applied AI workflows using modern tools and services.
4. Interpret AI outputs and communicate results to non technical stakeholders.
5. Assess ethical, legal, and organizational risks associated with applied AI systems.

## Module Structure

- Module 1: Foundations of Applied AI
- Module 2: Data Reality and Constraints
- Module 3: AI Tools and Models in Practice
- Module 4: Applied AI Workflows
- Module 5: Deployment and Operations
- Module 6: Interpretation and Communication
- Module 7: Ethics Governance and Organizational Risk
- Module 8: Applied AI Capstone

## Course Philosophy

 Students learn to make sound decisions about when and how to use AI in workplace settings. This course emphasizes practical application over theory. The focus is on judgment, clarity, and responsibility rather than technical depth.
