# Module 8: Applied AI Capstone

## Module Overview

This module synthesizes concepts from the entire course. Students design, justify, and communicate an applied AI solution for a realistic workplace scenario. The module emphasizes judgment, clarity, and responsibility rather than technical novelty.

## Module Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students can:

1. Design an applied AI solution for a realistic workplace scenario.
2. Justify design choices based on constraints, risk, and operational fit.
3. Communicate solution value, limits, and risks clearly.

## Capstone Structure

The capstone uses predefined scenarios to ensure consistency, fairness, and reuse. Students select one scenario and complete four required artifacts. All work builds directly on prior modules.

## Capstone Scenarios

Students choose one of the following applied AI scenarios:

1. An internal document assistant that helps employees find and summarize existing knowledge.
2. An operational forecasting assistant that supports planning and decision making.
3. A customer support triage system that assists human agents.

Each scenario includes a fixed context, known constraints, and realistic risk factors. Students do not invent scope.

## Capstone Artifacts

### Artifact 1: Problem Framing Memo

Students restate the business problem in their own words. The memo identifies the task, users, constraints, and success criteria. The memo explains why applied AI fits and where it may fail.

### Artifact 2: System Design Outline

Students describe the proposed system at a component level. Inputs, processing steps, tools, and outputs are explicit. The design reflects cost, data, and operational constraints.

### Artifact 3: Lightweight Prototype

Students implement a minimal working example. The prototype may use a notebook, script, or no code tool. Function matters more than polish. The prototype demonstrates the core workflow.

### Artifact 4: Stakeholder Communication Brief

Students write a short brief for non technical stakeholders. The brief explains value, limits, risks, and next steps. Language stays clear and direct.

## Assessment

The capstone uses a single rubric aligned to all course learning outcomes.

### Assessment Criteria

- The problem is clearly framed and grounded in realistic constraints.
- Design choices reflect sound judgment and tradeoffs.
- The prototype demonstrates a complete and coherent workflow.
- Communication is clear, honest, and appropriate for stakeholders.
- Risks and limitations are explicitly acknowledged.

### Alignment

This module assesses all five course learning outcomes.

## Course Closure

Students submit all capstone artifacts as a single package. Feedback focuses on decision quality and clarity rather than technical depth.
